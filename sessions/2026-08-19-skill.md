## One thing I'd forget by next week
- The name of this agent workflow architecture and what it entails: evaluator-optimizer, one of Anthropic's five workflow patterns. One model generates, a second scores it, the critique feeds the next attempt. LLM-as-judge is that pattern used for evaluation. It only holds while the scorer is independent of the generator: Panickssery et al. (NeurIPS 2024, arXiv 2404.13076) found models recognise their own output above chance and self-preference scales linearly with self-recognition, so anonymising the answers hides the label but not the style.
