## What broke and how it got fixed
- `docker info` returned `permission denied ... unix:///var/run/docker.sock`
  after install. Cause: the socket is owned by `root:docker` and the user was
  not in that group yet. Fixed with `sudo usermod -aG docker $USER && newgrp docker`.
