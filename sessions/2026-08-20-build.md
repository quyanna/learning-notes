## One thing I'd forget by next week
- The 262 missing artist records aren't random — they're a *kind* of show. Ticketmaster gives big touring acts structured attraction records with ids; small local bills, club nights and tribute acts exist only as free text in the event name. Calgary and Edmonton lose a bigger share of theirs than Vancouver does, so an id-based comparison makes them look emptier than they are — in the same direction as the hypothesis.
